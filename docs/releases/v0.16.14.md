# Kellie's Voice v0.16.14

**Android versionCode:** 54 · **Home Assistant integration:** 0.16.14 · **Release type:** stable with experimental, opt-in hands-free wake support.

**Headline: "Okay Nabu" hands-free wake-word support for Android TV.** This is an **opt-in** feature; you can still use the normal TALK button without any wake phrase. The app uses your existing Home Assistant Assist pipeline, with no required cloud model or bundled Ollama server.

## What's new

- **Say "Okay Nabu" to talk hands-free.** An on-device microWakeWord model detects the phrase. Say your request immediately afterward, for example **"Okay Nabu, turn on office lights"**, without waiting for a second prompt. Kellie's Voice opens its normal overlay and passes the spoken request to your configured Home Assistant Assist pipeline. Wake-word detection runs locally on the TV.
- **Faster responses after wake.** While wake listening is enabled, Kellie's Voice can keep a Home Assistant WebSocket authenticated and ready, without streaming microphone audio over that connection before detection.
- **Optional wake audio ducking.** Under **Settings → Voice & microphone → Wake word → Wake audio ducking**, choose **Off (default), 25%, 50%, or 75% quieter**. After the wake phrase is detected, the app attempts to reduce local media volume during the request and restore it when the overlay closes. The exact amount depends on Android volume steps; some HDMI-CEC or fixed-volume audio setups may ignore it. It **cannot** help the TV hear the wake phrase before detection.
- **Safer microphone controls.** Wake listening defaults **OFF**. Turning it on requires a deliberate action, a working microphone route, microphone permission, overlay permission, and a saved Home Assistant connection. A separate **Disable microphone capture** setting blocks wake listening; turning microphone capture back on does not silently arm the wake listener. **Forget Home Assistant login** also disarms it. Android's microphone-use indicator is shown while capture is active.
- **Remembered wake preference after restart.** When deliberately enabled in this version, the ON choice is retained after app updates and TV reboots. On supported Android versions, **opening Kellie's Voice after reboot automatically resumes the opted-in listener without toggling Wake word again**. Android 14+ restricts starting a microphone foreground service directly at boot, so the app may show a reminder to open it. **Zero-touch listening immediately after power-on, before opening the app, is not supported yet** ([tracking issue #163](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/issues/163)). Owners upgrading from an older experimental wake build must explicitly re-enable/resume wake once to approve the new persistence behavior.
- **Better organized TV Settings.** A remote-friendly settings hub groups Home Assistant, Voice & microphone, Overlay & appearance, Alerts & cameras, Screensaver, and Updates & account. Okay Nabu and audio ducking are found under **Voice & microphone**, with D-pad focus and Back navigation.

## Tested on hardware

The maintainer tested an experimental combined build on an **onn 4K Pro**: a single-breath "Okay Nabu, turn on office lights" command worked, YouTube media playback became quieter while Kellie responded and returned to its previous level afterward, **Disable microphone capture** prevented wake activation, and re-enabling microphone capture alone did **not** re-arm wake listening. Deliberately enabling the listener again restored the command.

**Additional testing recommended (and not yet fully verified on the final signed build):** automatic wake resumption when the app opens after a TV reboot, explicit OFF surviving reboot, Forget/revoked permissions, interruption and failure recovery, false activations, and behavior on other TV hardware. These results do not establish compatibility with every Google TV, Android TV or Fire TV device.

## Important limitations and privacy

- The wake-word engine needs a usable **16 kHz microphone input**. Some TV remotes expose their microphone only to Google Assistant; Kellie's Voice cannot take over Google's microphone button or its low-power hotword engine.
- While **LISTENING**, the microphone captures locally to detect the wake phrase. That pre-wake audio is not sent to Home Assistant. After detection, command audio is sent to the selected Assist speech pipeline, which may use local or cloud speech recognition. An accidental wake can therefore start an Assist capture.
- The warmed Home Assistant connection carries authentication and connectivity traffic, **not background microphone audio**. Turn Wake word OFF to stop always-on listening.
- A reboot or app update does **not** guarantee unattended microphone listening immediately at the Android TV home screen. A visible app launch may be needed. Avoid assuming a status of ON means the recorder is active; check for **LISTENING**.
- Advanced camera layouts, decoding and microphone behavior may differ by TV. No custom wake-word editor or alternative phrase is included in this version.

## Install or upgrade

1. Install the **official signed Android TV APK** from the v0.16.14 GitHub Release **over** the existing signed app from the published release; do not uninstall or erase its storage. Experimental GitHub Actions **debug** APKs have a different package name and are not replacements for signed stable builds.
2. Update the **matching v0.16.14 Kellie's Voice HACS integration** and restart Home Assistant. HACS and the TV APK are separate components. Do not install the HACS development version merely because the Android version has advanced.
3. Open the TV app and check **Settings → Voice & microphone → Wake word**. The default is OFF. Turn it ON only if you want local hands-free listening, then try **"Okay Nabu, turn on office lights"** with a device you have exposed to Home Assistant.
4. To hear YouTube lower during your question, choose a nonzero Wake audio ducking percentage. It takes effect **after** detection, and volume should return when the voice overlay closes.

For troubleshooting and detailed privacy behavior, see [Wake-word guide](../WAKE_WORD.md), [TV Settings guide](../TV_SETTINGS.md), and [issue #163](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/issues/163).
