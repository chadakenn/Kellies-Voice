# Kellie's Voice v0.16.11

**Release date:** October 25, 2026 · **Android versionCode:** 39 · **Home Assistant integration version:** 0.16.11

## Tested improvements on the maintainer's onn 4K Pro

- **Optional hands-free follow-up listening (#43, PR #114 and #116):** With local TV follow-up ON (8 seconds), a second question was answered without another TALK press. The recorder stopped on silence timeout and after STOP; Android's microphone privacy indicator cleared. Default is **OFF**, and these on-device tests do not cover all other TVs or every edge case.
- **Optional spoken TV announcements and independent voice volume (#110):** A 40% announcement voice gain sounded quieter both in the local announcement test and when Home Assistant sent a real `kellies_voice.announce` request with `speak`. Text announcements without `speak` stay silent. Audio ducking, all volume levels and reboot persistence need more hardware tests.
- **Post-reboot voice readiness (#44):** After reboot, the onn 4K Pro transitioned from Connecting to Listening and answered a request. Scoped TV alert recovery remains separate from the voice session.

## Other included changes needing additional testing

- **TV alert receiver recovery:** Incorporates the v0.16.10 HA restart retry and alert receiver diagnostics. If popups stop, use **TV → Settings → TV alert status → Resume TV alerts**. This recovered the owner's disconnected receiver, but other TV standby/network cases remain open.
- **Mic input compatibility (#112):** Fallback to 48 kHz capture and 16 kHz stream conversion when the device cannot capture directly at 16 kHz. CI tested; Philips and other hardware need direct validation.
- **Manual TV pairing (#69 / PR #111):** Explicit re-check for an entered pairing ID. Additional multi-TV manual-pairing tests needed.
- **Native Assist timer bridge (#57 / PR #113):** Optional and **OFF by default**; continue to use the documented Home Assistant timer scripts until the native bridge passes hardware QA.
- **Optional development update channel:** Stable updates still come only from signed GitHub Releases. Development builds require deliberate opt-in.

## Known limitations and tester requests

- **Camera voice reliability and latency (#39):** A camera can open immediately from the direct Home Assistant action while a local LLM may generate invalid JSON tool arguments or overflow its context; that is an upstream conversation-agent problem. Ensure the `TV alert receiver connected` binary sensor is on. Field testing of voice-open/close and camera popup delivery continues.
- **Other Android/Google TV and Fire TV hardware (#41, #71):** Microphone availability varies; remote Google Assistant microphone takeover is not implemented. Report TV model, Android/Fire OS version, input device, whether TALK transcribes, and whether overlays render.
- **Wake words, actionable TV alert buttons and screensaver Frigate dashboards:** Remain experimental branches or PRs and **are not included** in this release.

## Installation

1. Download the signed **`app-release.apk`** from this release and install **over** the existing signed app. Do not uninstall or clear storage. Stable `versionCode 39` supersedes signed `v0.16.11-dev.7` `versionCode 38`.
2. Download the matching **Kellie's Voice v0.16.11** Home Assistant custom integration in **HACS**, restart Home Assistant, then reopen the TV app to connect its receiver. HACS and Android APK are separate downloads.
3. Enable **TV announcements & cameras** and Android **Display over other apps** permission for popups. Follow [Installation instructions](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md), [Tester checklist](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/blob/main/docs/TESTING.md), and [documentation hub](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/blob/main/docs/wiki/Home.md).

**Privacy:** Never attach a Home Assistant access token, camera URL with embedded credentials, pairing identifier, or signing key to public issues.
