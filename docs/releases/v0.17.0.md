# Kellie's Voice v0.17.0

**Release notes prepared for the forthcoming public v0.17.0 build.** Publish only with a verified, permanently signed `app-release.apk` and matching Home Assistant `kellies_voice` integration version. This page is not proof that those artifacts have shipped.

## Highlights

- **Refreshed TV settings.** A two-column Compose Settings experience groups Home Assistant, voice/microphone, overlays, alerts/cameras, screensaver and updates/account. Multiple local themes and clearer remote focus are designed for the couch, not a touchscreen.
- **Home Assistant Quick Actions.** Configure TV shortcut labels, target entities and actions from Home Assistant. Icons and theme preview options are available, with behavior depending on paired integration/app capabilities.
- **Home Assistant dashboard screensavers.** A configured HA dashboard can appear automatically when Google TV enters idle mode. Improvements include trusted-network browser login handling, a TV-remote pointer with page scrolling, one-press Back exit, and removal of redundant Close/Select camera buttons *on HA dashboard pages only*. Frigate/native camera screens retain the controls needed for camera selection.
- **More reliable watcher startup.** The opted-in screensaver watcher attempts recovery after a full TV reboot, records boot/startup diagnostics and provides a visible-activity fallback on devices that restrict background starts.
- **Camera and alert improvements.** Existing direct Home Assistant services such as `kellies_voice.show_live_camera`, `show_camera`, `hide_camera`, and `announce` remain available independently of any LLM. The camera popup and live playback can be tested directly through Home Assistant.
- **Secure pairing development.** Owner-approved Home Assistant-first v3 pairing and renewable login have been tested in the dedicated QA setup. Its separate experimental `kellies_voice_v3qa` component is **not an ordinary replacement for the public HACS `kellies_voice` integration**; only claim public pairing support if explicitly included in and verified on the final shipped configuration.
- **Existing features remain.** Optional Okay Nabu wake word, Talk button, speech replies, timer and announcement overlays, Frigate/HA camera popups, live four-camera screensaver and TV audio ducking.

## Hardware validation and limitations

Physical **Onn 4K Pro** QA on the combined v0.17 build verified automatic HA Mobile Command appearance after full reboot without opening the app or manually resyncing; previously confirmed trusted-network dashboard login persistence, D-pad pointer scrolling, no unnecessary HA dashboard controls, and one-press Back exit. A direct `kellies_voice.show_live_camera` doorbell call bypassed the conversation LLM and visibly played live video.

The native four-camera fullscreen/audio flow was validated in earlier builds but **was not re-run end-to-end on the final combined v0.17 QA build at the time these notes were prepared**. Other Android TV models, including Philips devices, still need volunteer testing. Microphone availability depends on Android hardware; Google's remote Assistant microphone is not necessarily accessible to third-party apps. On Android 14+, rearming a microphone wake service at boot may require opening the TV app.

The maintainer's **separate Home Assistant LLM** sometimes hit a 20-tool-call limit while processing natural-language camera requests. This is an agent configuration issue; the direct HA live-camera path passed and does not depend on the LLM.

## Update and compatibility

1. Install the **official signed release APK over the prior signed stable version** without uninstalling or clearing app data. Do not replace it with an Actions `app-debug.apk`.
2. Update the **matching `kellies_voice` HACS integration**, then restart Home Assistant. The Android TV APK and HACS integration are separate components.
3. Leave existing opt-in microphone, wake-word, alert and screensaver preferences in place. Review Android permissions if the OS asks again.
4. For Home Assistant web dashboards, use a trusted TV account and a matching, properly configured HA origin. Do not paste tokens in web dashboard URLs.
5. Read [Installation](../INSTALL.md), [Beta/testing guidance](../BETA_TESTING.md), [camera screensaver guide](../FRIGATE_SCREENSAVER.md) and [troubleshooting](../TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

**Before publication:** verify matching versionCode/versionName, manifest version, signing certificate, packaged wake assets, release Notes, in-app updater and HACS distribution, plus owner approval to publish public `main`.
