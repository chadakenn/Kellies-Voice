# Kellie's Voice v0.16.7

Community test release, October 24, 2026. The owner requested continued
fixes and prefers shipping into the existing signed **in-app update** flow.

## Changes

- **#54 / merged PR #55:** Fixed focus of the ringing timer's Dismiss
  button for D-pad remote OK/Enter. Keeps focused-button selection when
  multiple named timers complete, with a guarded remote-key fallback.
  Back still dismisses ringing timers.
- **#79 / merged PR #81:** Added live-camera `show_header: false`,
  `image_fit: fit`, `refresh_seconds: 0 or 2..30`, and
  `duration_seconds: 4..180` to the Android app and matching HACS action.
  The placeholder camera still may periodically refresh while the HLS
  stream starts. It stops at the first actual video frame or on dismissal.
  True moving frames honor the requested fit mode.
- Carries forward the v0.16.6 alert-receiver heartbeat/recovery and
  v0.16.5 HDMI-safe video-decoder and snapshot changes.

## Update and test

On the TV open **Kellie's Voice → Settings → Check for app updates**.
Install v0.16.7 (versionCode 24) over the signed release; do not uninstall
or clear settings. Update Kellie's Voice in HACS to **0.16.7**, then restart
Home Assistant so the new live-camera service fields are recognized.

Please report the real-device results in [#54](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/issues/54)
(onn timer OK/Dismiss) and [#79](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/issues/79)
(Philips/onn live camera controls). Both automated Android/HA/HACS suites
passed for the component PRs, but their **physical hardware behavior has
not been confirmed** yet. Keep those issues open until verified. A six-second
popup may close before cold HLS starts; optionally enable Home Assistant
**Preload camera stream** with attention to extra resource usage.

Other open investigations, including TV alert reconnect [#78](https://github.com/chadakenn/Kellies-Voice/issues/78),
remain open for hardware verification. Do not share HA tokens, camera URLs,
or private video in test reports.
